FAQ · US visa questions

Questions, answered.

Which US visas does Sprint Overseas help with?

We specialise in American visas only: B1/B2 tourist and business visas and F-1 student visas. DS-160 form filling and visa appointment booking are also available as standalone services.

Is the first consultation free?

Yes. A 20-minute call maps your visa category, flags risk factors early and gives you an honest approval outlook, with no charge and no obligation.

Can you guarantee my visa will be approved?

No consultancy can: the decision belongs to the consular officer. What we control is the preparation: a complete, organised document file, a DS-160 that passes triple QC, and two mock interviews before your appointment.

How do you get an earlier visa appointment?

Our slot team monitors availability around the clock. When the calendar opens, we move first and lock the earliest date available at your consulate. Reschedules are included.

What does DS-160 form filling include?

Your DS-160 is filed precisely, reviewed three times, checked for photo compliance and confirmed with you before submission.

Do you help F-1 students with the I-20 and SEVIS?

Yes. From I-20 to interview day we cover SEVIS logistics, financial documentation and mock interviews.

How long does it take to prepare my visa file?

We collect, draft and polish every document and build your file within 48 hours.

How quickly will someone call me back?

Eligibility checks get a call back within 2 hours, Monday to Saturday. Consultation requests are called within 24 hours, usually much sooner.
